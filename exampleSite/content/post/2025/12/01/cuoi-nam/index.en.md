The end of the year is a good time to pause. Not to sum things up, just to notice.

Some things I did better this year than last: going to bed earlier, writing more regularly, not reaching for my phone the moment I wake up. Other things stayed exactly the same. Both are fine. A year does not have to be a growth report.

What stands out most is that the changes that stuck were all small. Ten minutes of reading before bed, one line in a notebook each evening, a walk after lunch. The big plans, for the most part, are still sitting in the notebook.

So next year's plan will be small too. Three things, no more.
